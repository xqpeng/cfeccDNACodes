
cfDNA_Feature/ 
             EGA5093_cfDNA_features.zip/:  five sub-directories corresponding to five types of cfDNA features of samples in EGAD00001005093 dataset
             EGA1275_cfDNA_features.zip/:  five sub-directories corresponding to five types of cfDNA features of samples in EGAD00001001275 dataset (An independent validation set)
Note: Five types of cfDNA features, including BPM(corresponding to directory BPM_CFDNA), EDM(corresponding to directory EDM_CFDNA), FSC, FSD, and CNV,
which were calculated by our previous developed tool of cfDNAFE (Cui W, You J, Jie W, Li Z, Peng X. cfDNAFE: Comprehensively extracting multi-omics features of cell-free DNA for noninvasive diagnosis. Methods,  (2025).).



cfeccDNA_Feature/ 
             EGA5093_cfeccDNA_features.zip/:  eight sub-directories corresponding to eight types of cf-eccDNA features of samples in EGAD00001005093 dataset
             EGA1275_cfeccDNA_features.zip/:  eight sub-directories corresponding to eight types of cf-eccDNA features of samples in EGAD00001001275 dataset (An independent validation set)
Note: Eight types of cf-eccDNA features, including BPM, EDM, SBM, OJM, JNM, OLR, CNV_im, CNV_onco,
which were calculated by our the developed R package, EccDNAFeature.
